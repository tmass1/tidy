Privacy

Tidy reads the words you select, so here is exactly what happens to them.

Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

  • Your draft goes straight from your Mac to Anthropic, with your own API key, when you ask Tidy to clean it up.
  • There’s no Tidy account and no Tidy server, and the app has no analytics.
  • Your key, history, modes and writing style stay on your Mac.
  • The app checks GitHub for updates by itself.
  • This website counts visits, without cookies.

The app

Your API key. It’s stored in your macOS Keychain and only ever sent to Anthropic.

Your selection. Tidy reads text only when you press its shortcut or choose a command from its menu. It presses ⌘C to copy your selection, then ⌘V to paste the new version when you press Replace. That’s what the Accessibility permission is for. Your clipboard is put back afterwards, unless you turn that off in Settings.

Your history. The originals and results of your last 500 cleanups are saved on your Mac. You can clear them in Settings, under History.

Your modes and writing style. They’re kept on your Mac, not in an account.

Accounts and analytics. There are none. The app doesn’t track how you use it.

When something leaves your Mac

These are all the connections the app makes, all of them over HTTPS.

WhereWhat’s sentWhen
api.anthropic.comYour draft, the mode or change you asked for, your writing style if it’s on, any character limit, and your keyWhen you ask for a cleanup or a change
api.anthropic.comRecent drafts, your edits, and any samples you paste, with your keyOnly if you turn on Learn my writing style or use Learn from these
api.anthropic.comYour key, to check that it worksWhen you add it
api.github.comA check for a new versionShortly after Tidy opens, then once a day
github.comA request for the new versionWhen you choose to update, or by itself if you’ve turned on automatic installs

Anthropic. You use Tidy under your own Anthropic account, so what you send is handled under Anthropic’s commercial terms and privacy policy. Tidy can’t see or change what Anthropic keeps. If a draft is too private to send, don’t run Tidy on it.

Update checks. Like any web request, these show GitHub your IP address. Tidy doesn’t have a setting to turn the check off.

This website

Visit counts. The home page and setup guide use PostHog to count page views and clicks on Download. It runs without cookies and doesn’t build a profile of you. This page and the terms don’t load it. To stop being counted in your browser, visit the home page with ?analytics=off added to its address. That choice is kept in one small cookie, set only because you asked, and it also covers Tommy Massaro’s other sites at tommymassaro.com.

Hosting. Cloudflare hosts the site and handles your IP address in order to deliver its pages. It also adds its own Web Analytics script to pages, which counts visits without cookies.

Fonts. The site’s typefaces are served from this site, so no font company sees your visit.

Selling and sharing

Your data isn’t sold, and it isn’t shared for advertising.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete data held about you, or to object to how it’s used. Tidy keeps nothing about you on a server of its own, so in practice this covers the website’s visit counts. What you send to Anthropic is covered by your account with them. Write to hey@tommymassaro.com and you’ll get help with it.
