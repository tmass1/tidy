How it works

Three keys. No copy and paste.

No chat window and no switching apps. Your draft stays where you’re writing it.

  1. Slack · #launch

    can u send the deck before 3

    Select your draft

    An email, a text, a Slack thread, a Notes page, a form in Chrome. If you can select it, Tidy can clean it.

  2. ⌥⌘C

    Tidy opens on top

    A cleaner version streams in within about a second. Edit it, switch modes, or ask for a change.

  3. Slack · #launch

    Can you send the deck before 3?

    Replace in Slack ⌘↩

    It goes back in place

    The new text replaces your selection, and your clipboard is put back the way it was.

See every change

Every edit, in plain sight.

Tidy fixes what needs fixing and shows you exactly what it touched, so nothing goes out that you didn’t mean to say.

Modes

Keeps itself current

Tidy checks for new versions and updates in place. Every build is signed and notarized by Apple.

Privacy

Your key, your Mac.

Tidy uses your own Anthropic API key, so you pay Anthropic directly for what you use. There’s no account to make and no Tidy server in the middle.

  • Your API keyStored in your macOS Keychain.
  • Your historySaved on your Mac only. Clear it any time.
  • Your modes and writing styleKept on your Mac, not in an account.

What you need

A MacApple Silicon, macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
An API keyFrom console.anthropic.com. Tidy asks for it on first launch.
AccessibilitymacOS asks once, so Tidy can copy your selection and paste the result.
